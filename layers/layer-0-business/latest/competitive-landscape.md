# Competitive Landscape

Where Harbor & Pine sits in its market and who it is compared against. No competitor or market research has been carried out yet; this document holds only what the client side and the team's reference material state, and the gaps are listed under Open Questions.

---

## Market Position

Harbor & Pine is an established seller of local tours and activities with operations in Lisbon and Barcelona. It sits between travelers and the operators who deliver activities, and coordinates guides, operators and day-of exceptions as well as selling. Its market share, trajectory and self-described position (leader, challenger or niche player) have not been documented.

---

## Key Competitors

GetYourGuide is the only named reference. It came up as a reference for ease of booking; no visual direction has been received. It is useful for common marketplace patterns but is not a complete model for Harbor & Pine, which also has to coordinate guides, operators and day-of exceptions.

| Competitor | What They Offer | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GetYourGuide | Tours and activities marketplace: discovery, booking, vouchers, traveler and supplier help centers | Ease of booking | ⚠️ Not yet researched |

Areas to review for GetYourGuide:
- Discovery by destination, date, activity type and availability.
- Activity details, including duration, language, accessibility and restrictions.
- Booking confirmation, vouchers, meeting points, changes and cancellations.
- The split between the traveler experience and supplier operations.
- How policies are communicated per activity rather than as one rule.

Questions to answer for GetYourGuide:
- How does it communicate booking certainty?
- What does it put first before and after payment?
- How does it explain day-of instructions and meeting points?
- How does it handle exceptions when the voucher is not the final ticket?
- What stays the supplier's responsibility?

Reference links: [marketplace](https://www.getyourguide.com/), [traveler help center](https://www.getyourguide.com/contact/), [supplier help center](https://supplier-support.getyourguide.com/), [general terms](https://www.getyourguide.com/c/general-terms-and-conditions/).

## Client Differentiators

⚠️ Hypothesis, not yet researched: Harbor & Pine's potential edge is its local operation — an Operations team in Lisbon and Barcelona that coordinates guides, operators and day-of exceptions such as meeting points, guide delays, weather changes and attendance. How much of this a marketplace leaves to suppliers is an open research question. Hotel partner relationships may be a second advantage. How travelers describe why they choose Harbor & Pine has not been gathered.

---

## Relevant Industry Trends

These are signals raised by the client side, not researched trends:

- **AI assistance across the trip.** The client wants an assistant that helps from planning through the day of the activity, starting with recommendations.
- **Link-first, no-download access.** Travelers and hotel guests expect a link that opens right away, without installing an app.
- **Hotel distribution.** Hotels are asking for branded booking experiences, which Sales sees as a potentially larger channel than direct sales.
- **Multilingual demand.** Hotel partners ask for English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Italian.

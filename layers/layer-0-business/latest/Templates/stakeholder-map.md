## Decision-Making Structure

Decisions are made informally in meetings, and no formal approval process has been described. The Executive Lead appears to be the sponsor and stated direction at kickoff: traveler-facing first launch, pilot languages from the pilot brief, no required download, no account to book. Approval responsibilities are not yet agreed. Scope and launch commitments still need alignment between Product and the Executive Lead. Operations has a strong say wherever operator agreements or day-of operations are affected.

| Decision | Who decides today | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall direction and sponsorship | Executive Lead (apparent) | ⚠️ Unconfirmed |
| Scope and launch commitments | Product and Executive Lead | Open — needs alignment |
| Design direction | Not assigned; the Executive Lead asked to see options for how a pending booking is shown | Open |
| Technical direction | Tech Lead provides feasibility input; no client-side technical owner identified | Open |
| Changes to a paid booking | Operations or the operator; how customer acceptance fits each type of change is not agreed | Partly defined |
| Customer expectation vs operator policy | Escalated today, "not always the same person"; final owner undefined | Open |
| Accessibility and privacy requirements | Not assigned | Open |
| Hotel concept for partners in Phase 1 | Sales deferred it until the main experience is clearer | Open |

## Key Relationships and Dynamics

The Executive Lead and Operations emphasize different problems — conversion versus the day of the activity — and Operations consistently anchors ambitions to operator reality: immediate confirmation only where availability is connected, no single cancellation policy, no autonomous AI changes to paid bookings, and "from scratch" not removing operator policies or tools. Sales and the Executive Lead differ on hotels: Sales floated hotels as the first audience; the Executive Lead kept the first launch traveler-facing and said hotels should not delay the pilot. The Executive Lead's eight-week expectation sits against a design-only signed phase, and the Tech Lead could not confirm a live launch in that window. Guides trust messaging today and would keep it until a new tool earns their trust.

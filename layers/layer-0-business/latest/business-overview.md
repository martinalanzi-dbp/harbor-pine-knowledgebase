# Business Overview

Who Harbor & Pine is, how it operates, and what its current reality looks like.

---

## Company Identity and History

Harbor & Pine is an established tours and activities business that sells local activities to travelers, with active operations in Lisbon and Barcelona. Its ambition is to help travelers "all the way through" a trip — discovery, booking and the day of the activity. The client describes itself through that vision rather than through features, and comes across as enthusiastic, direct and keen to move quickly. Company age, headcount, revenue and full geographic footprint have not been shared.

---

## Business Model

Harbor & Pine sells activities through a public website, through its own staff and through partner channels. ⚠️ Whether hotels already refer guests today is unconfirmed; the kickoff mentions "the group from the hotel," and the PRD treats hotels as a possible branded channel. Activities are delivered by operators, who control availability, prices, policies and delivery; guides often work for several operators at once. Where an operator's availability is synchronized, a booking can be confirmed immediately. Otherwise payment may be authorized while the operator accepts or declines the request by email or messaging, which can take up to 12 hours. Private tours can also depend on guide, language and price validation. After booking, the traveler receives a voucher that identifies the reservation, not each individual traveler. Operations handles assignments, changes, incidents, cancellations and refunds; cancellation rules follow each operator's agreement and differ by activity. The pricing model, margins and commission structure have not been shared.

---

## Customer Segments

Travelers are the primary segment and the audience of the first launch. They usually browse and book on a phone, and one person may book for a group. Hotel partners are a possible future channel: Sales believes a branded hotel version "could become bigger than direct sales." The first launch is traveler-facing and the hotel opportunity must not delay it; whether any hotel element belongs in the pilot is open.

| Segment | Description | Relative Importance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travelers (direct) | Book activities through the website, staff or partners; usually on a phone; one person may book for a group | Primary — first launch |
| Hotel partners and their guests | Recommend activities to guests; have asked for a branded version and five languages | Future — not in the first launch; pilot role open |

---

## Organizational Structure and Culture

The teams involved are the Executive Lead, an Operations team covering Lisbon and Barcelona, and Sales on the tourism account. The Executive Lead appears to be the sponsor and set direction at kickoff, but approval responsibilities are not yet agreed. Operational exceptions are escalated case by case, and "it is not always the same person" who decides. The client moves fast and thinks in terms of vision; Operations brings the operational constraints. The client has asked the team not to look at the current website so it does not bias the new direction, but shares process examples and anonymized booking messages instead.

---

## Existing Systems and Technology Landscape

Harbor & Pine runs on a mix of spreadsheets, email, messaging and separate support and payment tools, with no shared view across travelers, guides and Operations. Only 38% of activities have synchronized availability; the rest depends on operators replying by email or WhatsApp. Messaging is the channel guides trust most, but travelers do not always see later messages. No current-system audit has been completed. Operations cannot promise that operator systems will change with Harbor & Pine's, so the product must work with current operator agreements and tools.

| System | Purpose | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public website | Sells tours to travelers | Not shown to the team and not a design reference; stack unknown |
| Synchronized availability | Live availability for connected operators | 38% of activities in Lisbon and Barcelona; provider unknown |
| Spreadsheets | Availability and operations for unconnected inventory | Used for the remaining activities |
| Email | Operator confirmations; traveler and team communication | Updated messages are sometimes missed by travelers |
| Messaging (WhatsApp) | Operator replies; group lists to guides; guide coordination | Guides' most reliable channel; lists can arrive late or conflict |
| Support tool | Customer support | Separate tool; name unknown |
| Payment tool | Payment authorization | Separate tool; authorization precedes operator acceptance; name unknown |
| Vouchers | Proof of reservation | Identify the reservation, not each traveler |
| Customer records and call recordings | Customer history; inbound calls | ⚠️ Which systems hold them is unknown |

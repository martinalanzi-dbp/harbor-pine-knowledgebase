# Success Metrics

Product-level outcomes that show whether the product is succeeding. The client has mentioned more bookings and fewer support requests, and the PRD names candidate measures. No baselines or targets exist yet: the Executive Lead has no clean abandonment figure, and the only number available (38% synchronized availability) is a share of catalog activities, not a performance baseline. Every metric below is a candidate until baselines and targets are agreed.

## Pilot Readiness (proposed)

The PRD v0.9 draft proposes readiness conditions rather than numeric targets. These are not yet agreed:
- A traveler can complete the main booking journey.
- A guide can manage the group.
- Operations can handle expected exceptions.

Acceptable manual effort, supported exceptions, temporary workarounds and launch criteria still need agreement.

---

## Measurement Approach

The collection method, tools and review cadence are not yet defined, and no analytics or reporting setup has been described. Baselines come first: the Executive Lead and Operations need to share whatever figures exist for completed bookings, abandonment, support contacts and manual handling time before targets can be set. Who reviews the metrics, and how a missed target feeds back into product decisions, will be agreed with the Executive Lead and Operations.

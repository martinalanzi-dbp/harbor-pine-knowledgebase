Hi team,

Following the kickoff, we checked the active activity catalog for Lisbon and Barcelona. A few details to add to the discussion:

Only 38% of activities currently have synchronized availability. This is a share of activities in the catalog, not a share of bookings or revenue. The rest is handled through spreadsheets, email or messaging. Some operators take up to 12 hours to accept a request; private tours can also depend on a guide, language and price validation.

Authorizing payment does not mean the operator has accepted the booking. We still need an agreed customer message and a plan for requests that are declined. We are not approving a particular interface in this email.

Recommendations are fine, but changes to paid bookings require approval from Operations or the operator. We have not agreed how customer acceptance fits into every type of change. Please keep the approval flow open until we review examples together.

Cancellation rules differ by activity under existing agreements. We cannot assume a single platform policy. If customer expectations conflict with an operator policy, the final decision owner still needs to be defined.

Guides often use older phones with limited connectivity. For the day of the tour they need the group list, meeting point, language and emergency contact available without relying on a fresh connection. We also need to decide how a saved list is marked as outdated after a change.

The current voucher identifies the reservation, not each traveler. A reservation may cover several people who arrive separately. Do not assume that one QR scan resolves attendance for the whole group.

We can share anonymized booking messages and process examples. These do not require a walkthrough of the current website. Starting the platform from scratch does not remove existing operator policies or the tools we will still rely on during a pilot.

The pilot should not increase manual communication. Each exception needs an owner and a resolution path. We have not agreed the acceptable amount of manual work or the temporary workarounds.

These are operational constraints for the team's review. Scope and launch commitments still need alignment with Product and the Executive Lead.

Thanks,
Operations Team
